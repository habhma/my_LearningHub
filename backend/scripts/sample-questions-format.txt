Q1. What is the capital of India?
A) Mumbai
B) New Delhi
C) Kolkata
D) Chennai
Answer: B
Explanation: New Delhi is the capital of India and the seat of all three branches of the Government of India.
Difficulty: EASY

Q2. What is 2 + 2?
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 6
Answer: B
Explanation: Basic addition: 2 + 2 equals 4.
Difficulty: EASY

Q3. Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?
A) Mahatma Gandhi
B) Jawaharlal Nehru
C) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
D) Sardar Patel
Answer: C
Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution as he was the chairman of the drafting committee.
Difficulty: MEDIUM

Q4. What is the largest planet in our solar system?
A) Earth
B) Mars
C) Jupiter
D) Saturn
Answer: C
Explanation: Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, with a mass more than twice that of all other planets combined.
Difficulty: EASY

Q5. What is the value of π (pi) approximately?
A) 3.14
B) 2.71
C) 1.41
D) 1.73
Answer: A
Explanation: Pi (π) is approximately 3.14159, commonly rounded to 3.14.
Difficulty: MEDIUM
